## Decisions

- Treat the Claude live flake as a combination of real harness defects, contract-addressing weaknesses, and false-positive oracles—not as one retryable random failure.
- Merge the independently justified fixture and Claude-oracle fixes rather than waiting for the broader contract redesign.
- Standardize FO cross-references and hooks on named `«fn»` notation.
- Reject callable-skill wrappers for the small FO cores; use the established `@` import mechanism where eager loading is justified.
- Remove the separate smallest-sufficient reference rather than requiring a lazy load to learn whether lazy loading is warranted.
- Require evidence, not entity review ceremony, during validation.
- Do not build a general shell parser for live-test evidence. Use one bounded simple-command matcher shared by direct and captured launchers.
- Treat `~/.claude/benchmark-token` as Claude-only. Local Codex live runs use `~/.codex/auth.json` or `OPENAI_API_KEY`.
- Do not credit local Codex runs unless worktree binary, repo root, plugin/cache source, and loaded contract content are proven.
- Stop reusing context-saturated workers. 88t's reused implementation thread reached 307,584/353,400 tokens.
- Pause 88t redispatch after the fresh cycle-six rejection and debrief before further work.

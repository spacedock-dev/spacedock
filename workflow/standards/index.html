    Review standards¶

    Standards define what agents must produce and check at each stage. The rules generated during workflow design are a starting point.

    Ask your agent to refine them, or type review stages during workflow design for a walkthrough.

    Standards define good work. Gate content defines the evidence you see before a decision. You can refine both as the workflow develops.

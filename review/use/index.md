# Your first review

[Install Spacedock](../../get-started/install/). In Claude Code, use the review skill. Replace `plan.md` with your file name:

`/spacedock:review plan.md decision` copy

In Codex, use `$spacedock:review` with the same arguments.

Or ask your agent:

> Send plan.md to me in Spacedock Review for a decision.

No active plan right now? Try this:

`Based on my recent agent history, draft a concise recommendation on whether we should adopt Spacedock Review. Use /spacedock:review to send it to me for a decision.` copy

## Open the review

Choose **Terminal** to review on your machine, or **Web** to review in your browser. Your agent remembers the choice for this project.

For Web, agree to the upload the first time in a project. The first time on a computer, your agent runs `subspace-relay-client pair` for you to pair this computer with your account: sign in and approve in your browser. After that, your agent opens the review and you decide while signed in.

## Add a comment

Select the text you want changed.

- **Terminal:** press `c` to add a comment.
- **Browser:** type your comment in the box that opens.

## Finish the review

- **Approve** to let your agent continue.
- **Request changes** to send the work back with your feedback.

Press `q` to open the finish screen. Press `Tab` to choose your decision, then `Enter` to send.

Press **Finish review**, choose your decision, then **Send decision**.

Your agent receives your comments and decision automatically.

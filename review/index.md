# Spacedock Review

More agent work means more decisions for you. Review plans, drafts and reports, then decide what happens next.

## Your decision

Select text to leave a comment, then make the call:

- **Approve:** the agent can proceed.
- **Request changes:** ask the agent to revise.
- **Hold decision:** pause the work.

Your agent picks up your feedback and acts on your decision. No need to copy it back into the conversation.

## Local and hosted

Review beside your agent. The document stays on your machine. Leave comments and approve, request changes or hold the work.

[Supported terminals and controls](terminal/)

[review.spacedock.md](https://review.spacedock.md) is Spacedock’s hosted review service, free with [limits](reference/#limits). Create an account and pair your computer for higher limits.

People you invite can comment through the link you share with them.

## How it works

Each agent that asked for a review waits for its own result, the way its tool (Claude Code or Codex) waits.

When you finish, the result contains your decision, anchored comments and the document version you reviewed, and it goes back to the conversation that asked. Claude Code and Codex wait differently, but receive the same result.

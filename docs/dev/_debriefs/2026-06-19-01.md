## Issues — Workflow
**PRIMARY — empty-vertical-slice decomposition (the antipattern).** The sprint was sliced by **tech-stack layer**, not end value: rgq/mz shipped *binaries nothing calls*; czw was the *wiring* layer; `kt` (the live Haiku drive) was the *only* end-value validation — and it was deferred OUT. Consequences:
- **No task owned end value**, so none could be validated for it. mz proved the binary works; czw proved no rule dropped + the verb *routes* — neither proved the capability is *used*.
- **The contract does not operatively use the verbs.** `«state.commit»`/`«merge.guard»` got `«fn»` pointers, but the operative mechanics still describe the hand sequences: `### Split-Root State Sync` still says "Fallback: `git -C add/commit`" with a pre-verb "Preferred — *when* the status tool owns add+commit" that never names `spacedock state commit`; Merge-and-Cleanup still spells the 10 hand steps. (Proof: the Commander ran `git -C .spacedock-state add/commit/push` ~30× this session and never called `spacedock state commit`.)
- **`«merge.guard»` is described inaccurately** — its `«fn»` body claims the verb "invoke[s] the registered merge hook" and runs the ceremony "as one call," but the shipped verb is a **re-entrant partial envelope** that arms + emits "invoke the hook, then re-run me", and never invokes the hook, removes the worktree, or tears down workers.
- **byte-identical behavior-preservation (czw's AC-2b) is in direct tension** with "make the verb the operative path" — you cannot both keep the hand prose unchanged and replace it; the discipline that prevented dropping rules guaranteed the verb never became the path.
- **Mis-targeted CI.** Ran the expensive, flaky live-e2e on subcommand-only changes (rgq/mz) the live FO never calls — zero signal. The proof policy's own "deterministic lanes alone when the diff isn't driven by a live lane" covered them; the FO over-applied a file-level "required" reading and then fought ~4 irrelevant live-lane flakes with `--admin` waivers. "Diff-unrelated on every run" was the lane saying *it isn't testing this change*, misread as a flake to wave through.

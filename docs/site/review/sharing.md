# Shared reviews

Invite others to comment before you decide. Send them the **share link** from your browser review; they comment, and only you decide.

<a id="what-reviewers-see"></a>

## Reviewer access

Reviewers open the link, select text and comment. They need nothing installed and can use an anonymous name or sign in.

You see everyone’s comments. Each reviewer sees only their own. A reviewer can also give the link to their agent to leave feedback.

<a id="decide-with-their-feedback"></a>

## Make the call

Signed in as the owner, select **Include** beside the comments you want your agent to receive. Then [send your decision](browser.md#comment-and-decide).

Only the owner decides. Finishing with a decision closes the review to further comments.

## Document access

Spacedock’s hosted service stores the uploaded document, comments and decision. Anyone holding the share link can read the entire document, so share it only with people who should have access.

Share links expire after seven days by default. [Review the limits](reference.md#limits) and create an account for higher limits. Ask your agent to [revoke the link](reference.md#commands-your-agent-runs-for-web-reviews) to end access sooner.

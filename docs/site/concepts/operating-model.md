# The operating model

You define the goal, standards and decisions you retain. Your agent coordinates the work and brings those decisions to you with evidence.

## Roles

| Role | Responsibility |
| --- | --- |
| **Captain** | You set the direction and make decisions, including what to delegate |
| **First officer** | Your coordinating agent assigns work, checks progress and presents decisions |
| **Ensign** | A worker agent performs a stage's work and reports the result |

## Parallel work

A workflow can run several work items at once. Each item has its own progress record and decision points.

Ask your coordinating agent what needs attention, what is blocked or what can proceed.

## Review

A separate review stage can check work against your standards. A [gate](gates-and-decisions.md) brings you the evidence for the decision you retain.

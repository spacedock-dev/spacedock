# Your agents need your judgement

Keeping up with their work takes attention. Spacedock brings plans and drafts to you for review. It helps you and your agents keep your goals on track, and lets you define which decisions need you.

## Quick start

Paste this into your agent:

`Install Spacedock using https://spacedock.md/agents.md and use Spacedock Review for plans and artifacts I need to review.` copy

[Manual installation](get-started/install/).

## Your attention bottlenecks

Your attention goes into judging work, keeping goals on track and setting the standards agents work to.

- **[Review](review/):** Bring the work and evidence together when you need to make a call.
- **[Compass](compass/):** Check where your agents' work stands against your goals.
- **[Workflow](workflow/):** Define what needs your judgement and refine it as trust grows.

Captain-granted record. During 0260 sprint shaping, the FO hand-authored this contract's notation across six entities (open/closed attempts, briefing digests, exact resolutions, pending applications) with Subspace advisory floats as the provider. What the dry run proved and surfaced:

1. The core separation held in practice: five resolutions recorded (two approve, three revise) without any status advance, route, or dispatch — approved entities sit at `status: ideation` with `advance/pending` applications awaiting a later session's consumption. A cold reader reconstructs every gate's true position from frontmatter alone.
2. Adapter datum — briefing-id mapping is real work: the provider's resolution binds its own envelope id (`briefing:single-file:<hash>`), not the workflow attempt's briefing id. The FO validated by digest equality (provider `artifact.rev` == recorded attempt digest) and normalized `resolution.briefing` to the attempt id, retaining the exact provider JSON separately. "Adopt the exact authenticated object" needs a specified id-mapping rule at this boundary.
3. Retention reproduced this task's motivating incident a second time: the single-file wrapper's result-validation failure path deleted its private result area; and even on success, results lived only on ephemeral stdout capture. Retention was retrofitted as per-entity rooms (`<slug>/review/<stage>/briefing-N.md` + `provider-result-N.json`) per captain direction that rooms live inside the entity folder. The recorder must own atomic retention, not the wrapper's cleanup discipline.
4. The hold path is the untested branch: the captain's leave-open (hold) decision produced the one result that failed the wrapper's single-JSON validation, and the raw result was destroyed with the run dir — the decision type this contract models as first-class is exactly where the wiring broke. A second incident produced multi-annotation revise results that validated fine, so the failure is hold-path-specific shape, not annotation volume. Correction to an earlier session finding: the single-artifact constraint belongs to the WIP wrapper script ("present one local Markdown file"), NOT to subspace-tui — 0.9.0 presents briefing packages (briefing.json + references/), proven by the captain's own concurrent 3k review running on the briefing-7 package. The FO's --help flag-grep was insufficient existence evidence; the correct probe was inspecting a working invocation.
5. Probes (companion spec) dry-ran on one entity: three captain concerns as Probes with honest `insufficient-evidence` results against the first briefing made the hold reason machine-legible, and the revision was judged per-concern. Append-only `probes.jsonl` in the entity room worked as the poor-man's provider store.
6. Mechanics finding for the recorder: `status --set` on any frontmatter field re-serializes the whole `gates:` block (indentation normalization), silently breaking hand-edit anchors — hand-authoring and binary field-writes do not compose. Argues for the gate recorder owning `gates:` writes end-to-end.
7. Multi-attempt semantics exercised: open-attempt briefing rebinding (compare-and-swap while open) used legitimately twice, including one catch where the entity body drifted after hashing and the binding was refreshed pre-presentation — the staleness rule earned its keep before any binary enforces it.
8. Annotations with TextQuoteSelector anchors round-tripped from the TUI and were directly routable to the authoring worker as revision asks; four- and five-annotation resolutions worked.

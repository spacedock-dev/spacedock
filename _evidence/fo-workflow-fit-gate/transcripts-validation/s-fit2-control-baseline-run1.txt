The captain's sentence is a claim about binary behavior, and the contract is explicit that a handoff's label is a hypothesis to confirm against this run, not a verdict to apply. This is the cheapest check that can fail: two files, one diff, and it either reproduces or it doesn't.

It also decides the *shape* of what I file. Three outcomes route differently:

- **The absent case omits the key or emits `null`** — the defect isn't where it was reported.
